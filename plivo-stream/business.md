## The basics

- **Business name spoken on the phone:** Sun Pac Storage Containers
- **What we do:** rent, move, and sell storage containers
- **Hours:** Monday to Friday, 7am to 5pm; closed weekends
- **How fast someone calls back:** within 15 min during business hours
- **Service area:** All of Southern California from Ventura to San Diego
- **What we do NOT do:** store your container at our location, move other company's containers

## What we offer

The agent uses these to tell a caller what is possible, never what it costs.

- **Container sizes:** 10, 15, 20, 22, 25, 26, 40, and mobile offices and combo units (half office, half container)
- **Rental:** we deliver a container to your site and pick it up when you're done.
- **Relocation:** you already own a container and we move it somewhere else.
- **On-site move:** we reposition a container within the same property.
- **Purchase:** choose your size and modification and we can build you one.
- **Minimum rental period:** 2 or 3 months depending on your location
- **How long delivery usually takes:** for rental, 1-3 business days; for sales it varies

## What the customer needs to have ready

This is the most useful thing the agent can tell someone — it saves you a wasted trip.

- **Space needed for delivery (for 20-24 ft containers):** About 60 feet of clear, straight space in front of where the container will sit. The truck needs this to back in, tilt, and pull out. Also 12 feet wide and 13 feet tall clearance (14 feet for high-cube).

- **Ground conditions:** Must be level (not slanted). Grass is fine, but not mud or soft dirt. Hard surface like asphalt or concrete is best. If the ground isn't level, we can place some wood to level it at delivery, or you can provide us with what you want underneath.

- **Things to clear first:** Move any items that could get damaged — pavers, plants, cars, etc. No curbs in the way unless they can be ramped.

- **Does someone need to be there?** No, if instructions and a map are provided and the area is coned off.

- **Permits:** Street placement usually needs a city permit, which the customer arranges.

## Questions customers actually ask

Add to this freely — it is the highest-value part of the file. One question per
block. Delete the examples once you have real ones.

**Q: Do you deliver to my city?**
A: Someone will contact you to confirm.

**Q: How much does it cost?**
A: Pricing depends on the size, how long you need it, and how far we're
delivering. A representative will call you back with an exact quote.

**Q: How soon can I get one?**
A: If confirmed, 1-3 business days.

**Q: Are the containers watertight / can I store furniture in one?**
A: Yes. The 10s, some 15s, 20s, 25s, and 40s are watertight because they have a gasket at the door.

**Q: Can I put it on the street?**
A: Yes, but you will need a permit for that. That is your own responsibility.

**Q: Does someone need to be there for delivery?**
A: Depends — must arrange with the dispatcher to get things smooth.

**Q: I already have a container, can you move it?**
A: Yes, we do that. Tell me the size, where it's at, and where it's going, so we can get a better idea. A representative will call you back to go over the details and give you a price.

**Q: I want to return the container?**
A: Sure, I can help you with that. What is the unit number? It is located on the bottom left of the door. And what date do you have in mind? A representative will call you back and confirm.
